# 01 — Analyze existing evidence

## Objective

Build a complete CPU-only view of v2 and v3 without generating new model
outputs.

## Work

- Compare verified count, average score, speed, and artifact size.
- Break complete reports down by benchmark family.
- Identify tasks that change between v3-3,000 and v3-5,000.
- Separate quality gains from score-only gains and regressions.
- Record the result in `status.md` and the TypeScript handoff.

## Exit criteria

- A reviewer can decide whether the 8,000-step evaluation is worth its cost.
- No incomplete report is used as a quality claim.
